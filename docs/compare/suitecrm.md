# Contact Relationship Management and SuiteCRM

SuiteCRM is a mature open-source CRM, forked from SugarCRM Community Edition in 2013. Contact Relationship Management is open-source software that is not ready for production ([path to production](../../locales/en-us/path-to-production/index.md)). The two are not direct alternatives.

## Summary

| | SuiteCRM | Contact Relationship Management |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Category | Open-source, enterprise-ready CRM | Open-source demonstration software, driven by its specification |
| Licence | GNU AGPLv3 | MIT, Apache-2.0, BSD-3-Clause, GPL-2.0-only or GPL-3.0-only, at the user's option |
| Hosting | Self-hosted on Apache or IIS with PHP, or through paid hosting | Self-hosted: a Rust service, a Svelte interface and PostgreSQL |
| Focus | General CRM, maintained by SuiteCRM Ltd, with paid hosting and support | Sales, marketing, support and analytics, with recorded consent |

## Where SuiteCRM is stronger

- Mature and large. This project is a compact reference.
- Paid hosting and support are available from the maintainers.
- Longer history and a larger community.

## Where this project differs

- Small enough to read in full, with a specification that records why it works as it does.
- Stores a reference to a person, not a copy of their details, and requires recorded consent.
- Permissive licences are available.

## Suitability

- SuiteCRM suits organizations that need a mature open-source CRM with paid support.
- This project suits a compact design for study.
