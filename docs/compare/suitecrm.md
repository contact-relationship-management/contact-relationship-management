# Contact Relationship Management and SuiteCRM

SuiteCRM is a mature open-source CRM, forked from SugarCRM Community Edition in 2013. Contact Relationship Management is open-source demo software, not a production CRM, so this is a fair look at two different things rather than a contest.

## At a glance

| | SuiteCRM | Contact Relationship Management |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What it is | An open-source, enterprise-ready CRM | Open-source demo software, driven by its specification |
| Licence | GNU AGPLv3 | MIT, Apache-2.0, BSD-3-Clause, GPL-2.0-only or GPL-3.0-only, at your option |
| Where it runs | Self-hosted on Apache or IIS with PHP, or through paid hosting | You run it yourself: a Rust service, a Svelte interface and PostgreSQL |
| Focus | A wide CRM, maintained by SuiteCRM Ltd, with paid hosting and support | Sales, marketing, support and analytics, with consent first |

## Where SuiteCRM is stronger

- It is mature and large; this project is a compact reference.
- It has paid hosting and support available from its maintainers.
- It has a long history and an active community of forks and contributors.

## Where this project is different

- This project is small enough to read, and its specification explains why it works as it does.
- It keeps a reference to a person rather than a copy of their details, and puts consent first.
- Its licences include permissive ones, at your option.

## Which suits you

- Choose SuiteCRM if you want a mature open-source CRM with paid support available.
- Try this project if you want a compact design to learn from.
