# Contact Relationship Management and Zoho CRM

Zoho CRM is a commercial CRM with a free edition. Contact Relationship Management is open-source software that is not ready for production ([path to production](../../locales/en-us/path-to-production/index.md)). The two are not direct alternatives.

## Summary

| | Zoho CRM | Contact Relationship Management |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Category | CRM combining automation with an accessible interface | Open-source demonstration software, driven by its specification |
| Licence | Commercial, with a free edition for a small number of users | MIT, Apache-2.0, BSD-3-Clause, GPL-2.0-only or GPL-3.0-only, at the user's option |
| Hosting | Hosted by Zoho | Self-hosted: a Rust service, a Svelte interface and PostgreSQL |
| Focus | Sales automation, CPQ, forecasting and multichannel engagement | Sales, marketing, support and analytics, with recorded consent |

## Where Zoho CRM is stronger

- Supported commercial product with a free edition. This project is demonstration software.
- Includes CPQ, sales force automation and real-time multichannel engagement.
- A separate product, Bigin, is aimed at small businesses.

## Where this project differs

- The whole system can be self-hosted and inspected.
- Sending without recorded consent is prevented by design.
- Stores a reference to a person, not a copy of their details.

## Suitability

- Zoho CRM suits small businesses that need a hosted CRM.
- This project suits inspecting or adapting the software.
