Welcome to Contact Relationship Management Discussions

Discussions are now open for Contact Relationship Management, an open-source CRM for sales, marketing, customer support, and analytics. It has a Rust JSON API, a Svelte client, and a public specification. This is the place to ask questions, suggest changes, and share what you build with it.

## What this means for you

- You can ask a question without opening an issue, and the best reply can be marked as the answer for the next person.
- You can propose a change and discuss the problem before anyone writes code.
- Maintainers will post releases and changes of direction here, in Announcements.

## Before you start

The software is **not ready for production**. It runs on synthetic data, and no real email is sent unless a real mail mode is configured. The [path to production](https://github.com/contact-relationship-management/contact-relationship-management/blob/main/locales/en-us/path-to-production/index.md) explains what production means for this project and how far each part has got. Do not put real personal data in it.

## Where to go for what

- **Questions** about installing, running, or using it: [Q&A](https://github.com/contact-relationship-management/contact-relationship-management/discussions/categories/q-a)
- **Suggestions** for features or changes: [Ideas](https://github.com/contact-relationship-management/contact-relationship-management/discussions/categories/ideas)
- **Votes** on choices the project faces: [Polls](https://github.com/contact-relationship-management/contact-relationship-management/discussions/categories/polls)
- **Something you built** with or around it: [Show and tell](https://github.com/contact-relationship-management/contact-relationship-management/discussions/categories/show-and-tell)
- **Introductions and anything else**: [General](https://github.com/contact-relationship-management/contact-relationship-management/discussions/categories/general)
- **Bugs**, with steps to reproduce: [Issues](https://github.com/contact-relationship-management/contact-relationship-management/issues)
- **Security vulnerabilities**: report privately, as [SECURITY.md](https://github.com/contact-relationship-management/contact-relationship-management/blob/main/SECURITY.md) describes, never in a public thread.

Please be kind and constructive, as [CONTRIBUTING.md](https://github.com/contact-relationship-management/contact-relationship-management/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md) asks.
