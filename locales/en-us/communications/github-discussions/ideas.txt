How to suggest an idea for Contact Relationship Management

This category is for proposing changes and features before anyone writes code. A good idea starts with the problem it solves, so that others can judge whether the proposal is the best fix.

## How to suggest an idea

Start a new discussion in Ideas, one idea per discussion, and copy these headings:

```markdown
## Problem

What is hard or missing today, who it affects, and when.

## Proposal

What you suggest, in plain terms. Use a list if it has parts.

## Alternatives

Other ways to solve it, the workaround you use today, and what happens if nothing changes.

## Costs and trade-offs

What it would add in complexity, compatibility, maintenance, or risk.

## Open questions

What you want feedback on, such as "Would this solve your case?"
```

## Things to know first

- The [specification](https://github.com/contact-relationship-management/contact-relationship-management/blob/main/spec/index.md) is the source of truth. A change in behavior is made as a specification edit, a code edit, and a test edit together.
- The scope is set in [spec/scope.md](https://github.com/contact-relationship-management/contact-relationship-management/blob/main/spec/scope.md). For example, a contact stores a reference to a person in an identity service, not a copy of their name or address.
- Consent comes first. Nothing is sent to a contact without recorded consent.
- The open work is listed in [spec/tasks.md](https://github.com/contact-relationship-management/contact-relationship-management/blob/main/spec/tasks.md). Check it first; your idea may already be there.

## What happens next

A maintainer reads each idea and replies. An idea that fits the scope may become a task in the specification or an issue. A reply or upvote is not a commitment to build it.
